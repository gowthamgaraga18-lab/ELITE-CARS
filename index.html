06 About us

A showroom built
on trust.

Elite Motors began in 2009 as a two-bay workshop on MG Road. We still service every car we sell — we simply sell more interesting cars now.

Our buyers travel to auctions and private collections across India and the Gulf, and we turn down far more cars than we take. What remains is documented, photographed honestly and priced without theatre.

If a car is not right for you, we will say so. That single habit is why most of our clients are introduced by another client.

  • Inspected

    214 checks, photographed evidence, and a written report before you commit.

  • Documented

    Service history, insurance claims and odometer verified against manufacturer records.

  • Supported

    A named manager, a workshop that knows the car, and a 7-day exchange promise.
